# Gemini Simulation (Skills Bank)

Interactive training replica of the Gemini app (November 2026), for trainees who do not have Gemini accounts.

- **Explore:** the Gemini app opens directly: chat and Task mode, the + menu (files, Drive, image, video, music, Canvas, Deep research, Learn, Storybook), Library, Images, Videos, Scheduled, Customize (connectors and skills), Gems, Notebooks with Studio, personal intelligence, import memory, temporary chat, share screen, Gemini Live (voice with camera and screen) and theme.
- **Gemini in Workspace and Chrome:** Gmail (Ask Gmail, AI Overview, Help me write, Add to Calendar), Docs (Notes by Gemini, Help me write, insert answers), Sheets (answers, new tables, visual dashboard), Slides (create a deck, images, slideshow) and Chrome (ask about tabs, share tabs, Take over task). Switch apps from the assistant bar or the Google apps icon.
- **Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM; renamed July 16, 2026), updated in v7 to its November 2026 state:** open it from the Google apps icon. Home with notebooks and featured notebooks; Sources (upload, websites and YouTube, Drive, pasted text, Fast and Deep Research from the web or Drive, source guide); Chat that answers only from the sources with clickable citations, Configure chat (Default, Learning Guide, Custom), save to note and convert note to source, code execution for calculations on Pro and Ultra; Studio: Audio Overview (Deep Dive, The Brief, The Critique, The Debate; Shorter/Default/Longer; Interactive mode with Join), Video Overview (Cinematic on Pro and Ultra, Explainer with visual styles, Short), producer notes and Generate later for videos and slide decks, Mind Map, Reports (incl. Learning Overview), Flashcards, Quiz, Infographic (Sketch Note or Professional), Slide Deck, Data Table; Chat-only sharing, Analytics, settings; compute-based usage limits (refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap; Plus 2x, Pro 4x, Ultra 5x or 20x). In Gemini, type @Gemini Notebook in a chat to create a notebook or answer from one, or use + › More uploads › Gemini Notebook.
- **Assistant:** click any button and a card explains what it does, why it helps at work, and which plan has it. With live AI on, trainees can also ask free questions.
- **Plans:** Free, Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra (Egypt prices). Paid features appear only on plans that include them.
- **How do I…?** 10 guided how-tos in Egyptian Arabic: import memory, Gems to skills, notebooks, plus 7 for Gemini Notebook (build a notebook, ask with citations, podcast with Join, study tools, share as a team assistant, video with producer notes, use notebooks in Gemini with @Gemini Notebook).
- **Real AI:** answers, images (Nano Banana), music (Lyria) and videos come from the real Gemini API through the Skills Bank Cloudflare Worker. Add the secret `GEMINI_API_KEY` to the Worker and redeploy `simlab-worker.js`. Session codes are shared with the ChatGPT and Claude simulations.
- Limits (trainer panel): 1 real video per trainee per day and 15 per day for everyone, 6 images and 3 music clips per trainee per hour.
